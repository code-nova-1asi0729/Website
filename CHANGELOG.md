# Changelog

All notable changes to the Vigilia landing page are listed in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project uses [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).



## [0.3.2] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Privacy policy as its own page, in English and Spanish, linked from the footer.

### Changed

- Terms and conditions are now their own page instead of a dialog.
- Site files are organized into the `public` and `assets` folders.
- Product texts use "devices" instead of "equipment".

## [0.3.1] - 2026-11-13

### Fixed

- The complaints book, restored after a revert removed it.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added

- Complaints book with a form, opened from the footer.
- A "For you" link in the menu to the section per segment.

### Changed

- The section per segment is right below the hero, and says that each button opens the web app.
- The site URL is now https://code-nova-1asi0729.github.io/Website/.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- English and Spanish language switcher.
- About the Product section.
- A photo for each team member.
- Terms and conditions.
- Open Graph meta tags to share the page.
- A section with one button per target segment: building administrators, residents and maintenance companies. Each button opens its view in the web app.
- Video boxes in About the Product and About the Team. They show "Video coming soon" until the video is ready.

### Changed

- The team section shows the three current members.
- About the Product no longer links to the Sprint 2 video.

### Fixed

- An extra closing tag in the team section.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-08

### Added

- README.

### Fixed

- Color contrast, heading order and the FAQ animation.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-08

### Added

- First version of the landing page: hero, features, how it works, pricing, about, team, testimonials, FAQ and contact form.
- Buttons that open the web app. They replace "Request a demo".
- SEO meta tags.
- The four severity levels of the alerts.
